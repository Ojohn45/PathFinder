What is PathFinder?

PathFinder is not a course platform trying to teach you everything from scratch. It is a curated launchpad: pick a section and it points you to the best places inside it, the official docs, the free courses, the practice grounds, the documentaries, the sites people actually finish.

No more spending an hour searching "best way to learn Python".

Whatever you want to do today, PathFinder points the way.

Want to learn to code? Programming, from first steps to official docs. Bored tonight? Entertainment: anime, film, music and more. Learn something new Learning: science, languages, history, writing. Improve your daily life Lifestyle: health, money, cooking, fitness. Make something, or get work done Create and Work: design, business, tools. Read manga, manhwa and comics Read Comix: the best places to read, picked.

How it works

  1. Step 1 Pick a section

    Programming, entertainment, learning, lifestyle, create and work, or straight to reading. Each one has its own colour so you always know where you are.

  2. Step 2 See the best places, hand-picked

    Inside a section you choose a topic, and every topic opens the places worth your hours, not a page of search results.

  3. Step 3 Open one and start

    Tap a place and it opens in a new tab. PathFinder quietly notes where you went, so nothing is lost.

  4. Step 4 Come back and carry on

    Your history shows what you opened and when, on any device, so you pick up where you stopped.
